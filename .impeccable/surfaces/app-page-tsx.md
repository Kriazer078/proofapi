# Surface brief: ProofAPI landing and developer console

Scope: `/` (Persuade) and `/console/*` (Operate). Certificate, verify, journal and developers pages inherit the same world.
Audience: teams building AI agents for insurance, fintech and legal; lawyers and consultants as a second example. Recipients are non-technical.
Action: Start free (sign in with GitHub, get an API key) or Book a demo (GitHub issues contact).
Proof: live tamper demo, real certificate UI, market facts with sources (Grant Thornton 78%, Gravitee 7.2%, NAIC ~24 states), deployed Solana program, open source. No customers or testimonials exist.
Pricing: Start $0 (100 seals/month), Team $49 (10,000), Company by request; everything free in beta.
Direction: user-pinned by approval of mockups https://claude.ai/artifact/XYA5xVBpvNgtAYa74jTwam and https://claude.ai/artifact/YFJhbx2qufHAHngcBxSG39 (no concept roll; a user-pinned direction beats the roll).

## Direction contract

THESIS: An evidence desk. AI decisions are documents on a desk, and ProofAPI is the seal pressed onto them. Refuses the dark crypto-dashboard and the gradient SaaS hero.
OWN-WORLD: Cool grey desk (#f3f5f4), white paper sheets with hairline borders and soft long shadows, ink #0f1613, one saturated colour: seal green #0a7350 used only for verdicts, the stamp and the primary emphasis; red only for "changed". Literata display (pinned by approved mockup), IBM Plex Sans text and Plex Mono for record data only. Circular notary stamp with text on its ring.
STORY: The visitor sees an AI claim decision with a "Genuine" credential on it, understands logs are not evidence, sees how one request seals a decision, believes it through real numbers and a live tamper demo, and starts free or books a demo.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 45%: Literata headline "Prove what your AI did." with the verb in seal green, two-sentence lead, primary dark button "Start free" and secondary "See a certificate". Right 55%: a paper document (insurance claim decision by an AI agent) with an overlapping credential card carrying the stamp, three check rows and a dashed chip "fingerprints recorded on Solana". Nav: logo, How it works, Use cases, Developers, Pricing, Book a demo, Start free.
FORM: user-pinned mockup, position 1 of 1, seed key: none (user-pinned, no roll).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the live tamper demo, where editing the AI answer turns the credential from Genuine to Changed and the stamp re-presses in red.
Motion grammar: one authored moment, the stamp press (scale 1.2 to 1 with ease-out-quint) when a verdict resolves; everything else static.
